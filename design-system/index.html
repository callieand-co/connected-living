Colour

White walls, neon objects.

The interface is black and white. Colour is reserved for things you can buy — four collections wear a neon, on their objects and their glow and never behind text. Workshop and Voices wear black and white, like raw stock and newsprint.

Type

A couture serif, rendered in pixels.

Antilag is a fashion serif drawn on a pixel grid — the gallery and the internet in one letterform. It carries all the personality, so everything around it stays quiet: a mono for the interface, a clean sans for reading.

Rule

Antilag never goes below 40px. It’s drawn on a pixel grid — at display sizes the steps read as intent; smaller, they read as a rendering bug. Product names on cards use Geist instead.

Materials

Tile, lift, spray.

Three surfaces, borrowed and combined: a tiled shelf for every product to sit on, a soft lift that makes objects feel sculptural, and a blurred neon that shows up only where a collection wants to be noticed.

Tile

The tiled shelf

Every product stands on the same white tile wall and floor, so the catalogue reads as one gallery even before real photography exists.

Lift — hover me loopearplugs

The lift

Objects float with a real shadow and tilt as you approach. Enough depth to feel sculptural, never enough to feel like a render.

PhonesPhonesPhones

The spray

A collection’s neon, blurred behind its name like faded spray paint. One per page, maximum — it’s the loudest thing in the system.

Components

Built like a store.

Square edges, hairline borders, mono labels. The layout grammar comes from fashion product pages: a narrow column of facts, a big image, and prices treated as data.

Actions

Rectangles, not pills. The primary action is black and turns electric blue on hover.

Chips

A collection chip carries its neon. Intent chips stay monochrome.

Signature

The fitting room.

The stack builder, redrawn as a Y2K paint program. You dress a life the way you’d dress an avatar: pick a piece from the closet, and it goes on the figure. The paint palette is the collection picker — choose a colour and the room changes to that world.
